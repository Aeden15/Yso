1V1 KILL ROUTES
---------------

=== DISPATCH (Limb-Based) - PRIMARY KILL ===

Requirements for DISPATCH <target>:
1. Target must be PRONE (from Sweep)
2. At least ONE LEG broken (100%+ damage) (to keep prone)
3. HEAD broken/damaged (100%+ or damagedhead affliction)
4. WINDPIPE damaged (from Needle - damagedwindpipe or crushedthroat)

CRITICAL RULE: NEVER break legs until head is also prepped!
Breaking legs prematurely wastes your setup.

Phase 1 - Leg Prep (Willow -> Rain Form):
- Goal: Both legs to ~90.8% (one Kuro from breaking)
- Use: Kuro left, Kuro right to balance prep
- Protection: Use LIGHT modifier on prepped legs to avoid breaks
- Transition: Rain -> Oak when kata >= 5 and legs prepped
- Note: Rain has 24 kata capacity = ~8 combos of sustained prep

Phase 2 - Head Prep (Oak Form):
- Goal: Head to ~92% (one hit from breaking)
- Use: NERVESTRIKE (paralysis prevents parrying!) then Risingkick head
- Order: Staff attacks FIRST (Nervestrike + staff), then kick
  Example: COMBO <target> nervestrike kuro left risingkick head
- Paralysis from Nervestrike stops them parrying your head attacks
- Transition: Oak -> Gaital when BOTH legs AND head prepped

Phase 3 - Kill (Gaital Form):
- Combo 1: SWEEP + KURO (prones + breaks both legs)
  Syntax: COMBO <target> sweep flashheel left
- Combo 2: Flashheel right + NEEDLE + NEEDLE (breaks head + windpipe
damage, plus breaks other leg)
  Syntax: COMBO <target> spinkick needle needle
- Combo 3: DISPATCH <target> (instant kill)
- Could also Spinkick to raise head from 1 to 2 depending on Kata.

If kill fails: Gaital -> Maelstrom -> Oak (cycle back for more prep)


=== LOCK ROUTE (Affliction-Based) ===

Goal: Build to riftlock and then truelock, then kill through sustained
damage pressure.

Lock Progression:
  Softlock:   asthma + anorexia + slickness
  Venomlock:  softlock + paralysis
  Riftlock: Both arms broken, addiction, softlock into hard lock
  Hardlock:   venomlock + impatience (Telepathy)
  Truelock:   hardlock + weariness (blocks Fitness)

Shikudo Affliction Sources:
- Hiraku (Willow): anorexia + stuttering
- Livestrike (Oak/Maelstrom): asthma
- Ruku torso (Rain/Oak/Gaital/Maelstrom): slickness
- Nervestrike (Oak): paralysis
- Kuro (Rain/Oak/Gaital): weariness/lethargy

Telepathy Integration:
- Establish MINDLOCK first (required for other Telepathy)
- MIND IMPATIENCE for hardlock (use in Rain for speed bonus!)
- MIND BATTER for stupidity + epilepsy + dizziness stack

Form Strategy:
1. Willow: Hiraku for anorexia
2. Rain: Ruku torso for slickness, build kata
3. Oak: Livestrike for asthma, Nervestrike for paralysis
4. Stay in Oak/Rain: Mind Impatience for hardlock, maintain afflictions

Kill Method: Pure damage pressure on truelocked target.


=== RIFTLOCK (Blackout Burst) - ADVANCED ===

The Riftlock combines hidden Blackout bursts with affliction pressure.
Opponent sees NOTHING during Blackout - they must DIAGNOSE to know
state.

Setup Phase (Oak):
- Build hindrance: Nervestrike (paralysis), Ruku (clumsiness)
- Establish mindlock
- Transition: Oak -> Willow -> Rain when para + clumsy established

Prep Phase (Rain):
- Build to 9+ kata
- Optional: Additional afflictions with Kuro (weariness) and Hiru
(dizziness)

Blackout Burst (Rain at 9+ kata):
1. MIND BLACKOUT (opponent goes blind)
2. MIND PARALYSE (stops parry - HIDDEN)
3. Frontkick + Kuro + Ruku burst (all HIDDEN)
   Example: COMBO target frontkick left kuro left ruku right

Result State After Burst:
- Limbs: Both arms damaged, one leg damaged
- Afflictions: paralysis, clumsiness, healthleech, weariness, lethargy

Continuation (Oak):
1. Transition to Oak
2. Livestrike for asthma (buried in kelp stack)
3. Ruku torso for slickness
4. Nervestrike for paralysis refresh
5. MIND IMPATIENCE for hardlock
6. Continue pressure or transition to Gaital for Dispatch

Psychology: Most opponents assume you're just disrupting during Blackout
and will CONCENTRATE. Meanwhile you've paralyzed them, burst limbs, and
stacked afflictions. When Blackout ends, they're in terrible state with
ZERO information about what happened.


=== SCYTHE FORK (Gaital) - ADVANCED ===

Fork the opponent into choosing between head or leg death.

Setup Combo:
1. SWEEP + break right leg to level 2 (prone + leg damage)
2. SPINKICK + break left leg to level 3 + crushed throat
3. Hit right leg to level 2

Enemy Forced Choices:

If they MENDING HEAD (for crushed throat):
- Counter: If they restoration leg, transition Rain and push for Scythe

If they RESTORATION HEAD (level 3):
- Counter: Needle Dispatch is now available

If they HOLD SALVE:
- Counter: Mind Impatience, Mind Batter on repeat to pressure Scythe

Outcome: They pick head or leg first, or die to Scythe.
