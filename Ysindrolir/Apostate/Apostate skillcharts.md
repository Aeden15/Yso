# Apostate skill references

- [Evileye skillchart](Evileye%20skillchart.md): 34 detailed AB entries plus
  index-only Truestare; command/AK affliction mappings and Curseward notes.
- [Apostasy skillchart](Apostasy%20skillchart.md): 20 supplied abilities, their
  requirements, affliction outcomes, summon effects, and Eliminate channel notes.
- [Necromancy skillchart](Necromancy%20skillchart.md): 14 detailed entries plus
  partial Essence; afflictions, resource recovery, movement and Vivisect requirements.
- [Necromancy: Shrivel](Necromancy%20Shrivel%20reference.md): four existing limb
  affliction scores and handling of side-specific versus unsided responses.

Structured, reference-only package data is grouped by skillset:

- `Yso.ref.apostate.evileye` — `evileye_reference.lua`
- `Yso.ref.apostate.apostasy` — `apostasy_reference.lua`
- `Yso.ref.apostate.necromancy` — `necromancy_reference.lua`

The provided in-game AB captures and user clarifications take precedence over
conflicting web descriptions. Fields missing from a capture remain unspecified.
Standard demon summons require a drawn pentagram; Bloodpact's captured setup
creates a pentagram and selected demon using fresh blood on the daegger.

These charts do not activate offense. Deadeyes automation and automatic
Eliminate/Vivisect are not added by the reference files.
