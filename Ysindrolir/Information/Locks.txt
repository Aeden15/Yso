Softlock

Asthma - prevents smoking pipes, cured by eating Aurum/Kelp
Anorexia - prevents eating herbs, cured by applying Epidermal/Focusing
Slickness - prevents applying salves, cured by eating
Magnesium/Bloodroot or smoking Realgar/Valerian



Venomlock

Paralysis - prevents movement and touching their Tree tattoo, cured by
eating Magnesium/Bloodroot
Asthma - prevents smoking pipes, cured by eating Aurum/Kelp
Anorexia - prevents eating herbs, cured by applying Epidermal/Focusing
Slickness - prevents applying salves, cured by eating
Magnesium/Bloodroot or smoking Realgar/Valerian



Truelock/Hardlock

Paralysis - prevents Tree tattoo, cured by eating Magnesium/Bloodroot
Asthma - prevents smoking pipes, cured by eating Aurum/Kelp
Anorexia - prevents eating herbs, cured by applying Epidermal/Focusing
Slickness - prevents applying salves, cured by eating
Magnesium/Bloodroot or smoking Realgar/Valerian
Impatience - prevents using Focus, cured by eating Plumbum/Goldenseal

Also includes various other afflictions, such as Weariness, Stupidity,
Recklessness, etc., which prevent class-specific active and passive
cures



Focuslock

Paralysis - prevents Tree tattoo, cured by eating Magnesium/Bloodroot
Asthma - prevents smoking pipes, cured by eating Aurum/Kelp
Anorexia - prevents eating herbs, cured by applying Epidermal/Focusing
Slickness - prevents applying salves, cured by eating
Magnesium/Bloodroot or smoking Realgar/Valerian
In lieu of Impatience, the user afflicts multiple mental afflictions in
the hopes that when the opponent uses Focus it cures something other
than anorexia



Riftlock

Two arm breaks - prevents out-rifting herbs, cured by apply mending to
arms
Slickness - prevents applying salves, cured by Magnesium/Bloodroot or
smoking Realgar/ Valerian
Asthma - prevents smoking pipes, cured by eating Aurum/Kelp
Addiction (Vardrax) can help with a Riftlock, as it will force your
opponent to eat everything already in their hands when their arms are
broken



Salvelock

Two arm breaks, but at level 2 or higher - prevents out-rifting herbs,
cured by apply mending to arms, and level 2 breaks will mean they cannot
use Restore to heal their limbs.
Slickness - prevents applying salves, cured by Magnesium/Bloodroot or
smoking Realgar/Valerian
Asthma - prevents smoking pipes, cured by eating Aurum/Kelp
Addiction (Vardrax) can help with a Riftlock, as it will force your
opponent to eat whatever is already in their hands when their arms are
broken



Sleeplock

Sleep/Delphinium afflicted at least three times in very rapid
succession. The first will strip the Insomnia affliction, which prevents
falling asleep. The second strips the Gypsum/Kola defence, which allows
a person to wake instantly. The third puts the opponent to sleep, which
would then take long enough to wake from, that you can keep afflicting
Sleep/Delphinium, or continue with other locking afflictions.



Aeonlock

Aeon - allows only one action at a time on a lengthy balance.
Asthma - prevents curing Aeon by smoking Cinnabar/Elm
Further Aurum based afflictions to ensure Asthma remains stuck.
